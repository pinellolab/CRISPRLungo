The whole analysis runs in this browser — nothing is uploaded. Leaving the page stops it.

Prime assembly analysis

Every read is compared with the wild-type, precise-PA, dual-flap, 5′-flap and 3′-flap alleles and assigned to the one that explains the most of it. The table, the pie charts and the allele-plot labels below are that classification; outcome names follow Levesque et al., Nature 2026. The per-position insertion, deletion and substitution plots stay on the wild-type reference, since PA has no equivalent of them.

Alignment

The same alignment the CLI writes for IGV: one row per read, rebuilt from the reference and the mutations that passed filtering. Red dashed = cleavage site · blue band = protospacer · a deletion is the joined, labelled stretch (red over the large-deletion cutoff) · purple tick = insertion, with its length written beside it when it is over the large-insertion cutoff — click a tick to see the inserted sequence · substitutions are coloured by the base that replaced the reference (A green, C blue, G amber, T red) · green hatched = inverted segment. Untick "Filtered mutations only" for the raw aligner output. In PA mode a reference selector appears on the left, switching between the wild-type reference and the precise-PA allele, whose dashed marks are the two cleavage sites, the two flap boundaries and any donor fragment junctions. The category selector beside it narrows the view to one class of read — a PA outcome when PA mode ran, or one of the standard classes — which is easier to read than scrolling for a band. Drag to pan, ctrl or shift with the wheel to zoom; the box scrolls through the stacked reads.
